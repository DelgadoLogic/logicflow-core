If you are a consumer habitually residing in the European Union (EU), the European Economic Area (EEA), or the United Kingdom (UK), the EU Consumer Rights Directive (Directive 2011/83/EU, Articles 9 and 16(m)) and the UK Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 (Regulations 29 and 37) provide a statutory fourteen (**14**) calendar day cooling-off period to withdraw from a distance contract without giving any reason.
